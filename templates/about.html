About the platform

Designed to make symptom-based guidance easier to understand.

MediPredict combines machine learning with structured health guidance so users can move from scattered symptoms to a clearer, more actionable overview.

Educational use Guided symptom input Top-match predictions

Our mission

Health guidance that feels approachable, not overwhelming.

We built this project to make symptom-based health exploration more usable for everyday people. Instead of returning only a raw prediction, the app also provides context such as likely condition matches, precautions, activity guidance, dietary support, and prompts for when it is time to contact a doctor.

The goal is not to replace clinical diagnosis. The goal is to help users organize what they are experiencing, understand possible next steps, and approach care conversations more confidently.

What it includes

Condition predictions

Top 3 likely matches generated from supported symptom combinations.

Safety messaging

Care escalation guidance and emergency warning signs are surfaced with each result.

Support guidance

Diet, medication, workout, and precaution suggestions are pulled from the project datasets.

Vision

Better health literacy

Support people with understandable explanations instead of opaque technical outputs.

Approach

Structured and guided

Improve prediction quality by combining autocomplete symptom selection with a reproducible ML pipeline.

Responsibility

Educational only

This platform is built to inform, not diagnose, and should always sit behind professional medical advice.
